Our Heritage & Story

A Legacy of Healing & Technological Innovation

Established in 1999 with a foundational mission to make international-standard medical care accessible to every citizen, MZH Hospisol has grown from a humble 50-bed community clinic into a benchmark 500+ bed tertiary healthcare powerhouse.

Our state-of-the-art campus brings together over 200 world-class surgical specialists, medical oncologists, interventional cardiologists, and pediatric intensivists. Equipped with AI-assisted diagnostics, 3T MRI, 128-slice CT, and robotic surgery, we deliver treatment with unparalleled clinical accuracy.

Above all, we maintain an unwavering commitment to patient dignity, transparency, and warmth — treating every individual not just as a patient, but as family.

Help & FAQs

Frequently Asked Questions

Answers to common queries about hospital admissions, emergency protocol, and insurance partnerships.

What insurance providers and cashless TPA facilities do you accept?
MZH Hospisol is empaneled with over 35+ major health insurance companies and all leading Third-Party Administrators (TPAs), including Star Health, HDFC ERGO, ICICI Lombard, Max Bupa, Medi Assist, and CGHS/ECHS. We offer 24/7 dedicated cashless hospitalization desks to facilitate smooth pre-authorization and discharge.
How can I reach out to a medical specialist?
You can reach out through our Contact Portal, call our 24/7 helpline at +91 123 456 7890, or visit our hospital information desk from Monday through Saturday (9:00 AM to 7:00 PM).
What emergency services and trauma support are available?
Our Level 1 Trauma Center, Emergency Resuscitation Unit, Cardiac Cath Lab, and Advanced Cardiac Life Support (ACLS) ambulances are operational 24 hours a day, 365 days a year. Our emergency response team includes board-certified trauma surgeons, emergency physicians, and critical care intensivists.
Are diagnostic test reports available online?
Yes! All pathology, biochemistry, and radiology reports (MRI, CT, X-ray, Ultrasound) are securely delivered via SMS and email with an online digital health portal login for easy download, sharing, and storage.
