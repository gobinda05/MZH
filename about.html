Our Story

Pioneering Healthcare With Heart & Innovation

Founded in 1999, MZH Hospisol has grown from a modest 50-bed facility to a comprehensive 500+ bed multi-specialty hospital that proudly serves over 1 million patients annually. Our journey has been marked by an unwavering commitment to combining compassionate care with cutting-edge medical technology.

We bring together the finest medical minds, the latest technology, and a patient-first approach to deliver healthcare that truly makes a difference. Our hospital is equipped with advanced robotic surgery systems, AI-powered diagnostics, comprehensive digital health records, and state-of-the-art medical equipment.

Today, MZH Hospisol stands as a beacon of hope and healing, recognized nationally and internationally for our excellence in patient care, medical innovation, and research contributions.

Our Mission

To provide compassionate, high-quality healthcare services that improve the health and well-being of our patients and community through excellence in clinical care, education, and research.

Our Vision

To be the most trusted and preferred healthcare provider, recognized for clinical excellence, innovation, and patient-centered care that transforms lives and sets new standards in medical practice.

Our Values

Compassion, Integrity, Excellence, Innovation, Respect, Teamwork, and Safety - guiding every decision we make and every action we take in service of our patients and community.
