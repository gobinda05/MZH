About MZH Hospisol

Pioneering Healthcare With Heart & Innovation

Founded in 1999, MZH Hospisol has grown from a 50-bed healthcare facility into a 500+ bed premier multi-specialty tertiary care hospital serving over one million patients annually.

We combine human compassion with state-of-the-art medical technology, including Da Vinci robotic surgical systems, 3T MRI, 128-slice CT scans, and fully automated diagnostic pathology.

Global gold standards in patient safety & clinical protocols.
