Founded in 1999, MZH Hospisol has grown from a 50-bed facility to a 500+ bed multi-specialty hospital that serves over 1 million patients annually. Our commitment to combining compassionate care with cutting-edge technology has made us a leader in the healthcare industry.

We bring together the finest medical minds, the latest technology, and a patient-first approach to deliver healthcare that truly makes a difference. Our hospital is equipped with advanced robotic surgery systems, AI-powered diagnostics, and digital health records.
